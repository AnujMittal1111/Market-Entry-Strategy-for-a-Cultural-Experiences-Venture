Overview

This project presents a comprehensive market entry strategy for launching a premium cultural experiences venture in India. After evaluating multiple cultural sectors, Craft & Textile Arts emerged as the most commercially attractive opportunity due to its strong demand, scalability, rich artisan ecosystem, and potential for recurring revenue streams.

The proposed venture combines immersive cultural experiences, online learning, and commerce into a unified business model designed for sustainable growth and long-term competitive advantage.

Problem Statement

India possesses over 3,000 GI-tagged crafts and a vast artisan ecosystem, yet cultural experiences remain fragmented, localized, and largely unorganized.

Current market gaps include:

Lack of a national branded cultural experience company
Limited year-round engagement by existing players
Weak integration of experiences, learning, and commerce
Underutilization of artisan talent and heritage assets

This creates a significant opportunity to build a scalable cultural experience platform.
